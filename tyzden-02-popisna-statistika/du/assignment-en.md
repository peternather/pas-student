# Homework 2 (optional): How much does a New York taxi cost?

**Deadline:** before lecture 3 · **Points:** up to 10 bonus · **Time:** ~2 hours

A taxi company wants to write on its website: *"A typical ride with us costs $X and takes Y minutes. Our customers tip around Z %."* Using a sample of 6,433 real rides from March 2019, find out which numbers to publish and justify them.

## Steps

1. Open the notebook [`homework-en.ipynb`](homework-en.ipynb) in Google Colab.
2. Work through tasks 1–5, fill in every `...` and the ✍️ answers.
3. Run the whole notebook: **Runtime → Run all**. The last cell must print an `ANSWERS_JSON` line.
4. Submit the `.ipynb` file (*File → Download → Download .ipynb*).

## Grading

- 4 pts: automatic check of eight numeric answers,
- 6 pts: justifications and conclusion (lecturer).

**Note:** the data are not perfectly clean, and one thing in them is not what it seems. Finding it is part of the task.
