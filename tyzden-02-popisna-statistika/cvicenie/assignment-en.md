**Goal:** practise the lecture's recipe on different data: variable types, centre, spread, outliers, comparing groups and a conclusion.

Open the notebook [`exercise-en.ipynb`](exercise-en.ipynb) in Google Colab and work alone or in pairs. The `tips` dataset (244 bills from one restaurant) is loaded directly in the notebook.

| Part | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | variable types |
| 2 | bill total: mean, median, spread, histogram |
| 3 | tip percentage and outliers (1.5 × IQR rule) |
| 4 | comparing days (box plot, `groupby`) |
| 5 | smokers vs non-smokers: centre and spread |
| 6 | open question: which day is best for a waiter? |
